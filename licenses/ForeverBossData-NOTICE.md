# Forever boss audit data

Aeternum contributors matched public observations by NPC, item and encounter ID, reconciled roster aliases, classified the evidence and compared route positions with recorded pulls. Aeternum’s adaptations of the player observation data in `docs/boss-audit` and the bundled boss data are shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The addon's code is covered by the repository license.

- **Efficient Games** supplies the EG Link combat and loot observations under CC BY 4.0. Aeternum filters by product, build, hotfix window and source type, computes distances from recorded pulls, and matches in-instance boss casts to Forever spell records. [Efficient Games](https://efficient.games) · [Dataset and license](https://github.com/JIVESCORP/eg-link-output-wowf/tree/c1bf99bc2835bbe1b2dad82fb99f994c0cfffe33)
- **Drop Tables contributors** share their submitted loot counts under CC BY-SA 4.0. Aeternum records whether a source observed an item. [Drop Tables contributors and license](https://github.com/valcheffnet/DropTables/blob/c68b7472291bd790b4b9089ac8753cd9e7d51945/LICENSE-DATA)
- **Everloot contributors** share their public corpus under CC BY-SA 4.0. Drop Tables converted the corpus to counts per install and filtered pre-launch and specified pre-hotfix records. Aeternum uses the item IDs seen in creature loot windows. [Everloot](https://everloot.app) · [Drop Tables import and attribution](https://github.com/valcheffnet/DropTables/blob/c68b7472291bd790b4b9089ac8753cd9e7d51945/tools/import_everloot.py)
- **AllTheThings WoW Addon** publishes its Camelot instance catalogue under the MIT license. [Pinned source](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/tree/789b0feb395396c181999063b691f854167595c6) · [MIT license](AllTheThings-MIT.txt)
- **EzWoW Dungeon Route Guide** provides a Forever dungeon catalogue and map references. Many of its map coordinates derive from CMaNGOS Classic-DB under GPLv3. Aeternum compares the references where a world-coordinate transform exists. [Pinned data notice](https://github.com/ezwow-addons/EzWoW-DungeonRouteGuide/blob/b81ba9ce16c494d02a7fc518e716cc34da19592c/LICENSE-DATA-NOTICE.md)
- **Forever Scout contributors** supplied the credited player reports on Forever Guide. The audit records factual NPC and item IDs from 28 public dungeon pages. **Exehn's Dungeon Journal 2.0.10**, as displayed by WoWCensus, also identifies the optional Tunnelweb Matriarch. [Forever Guide](https://guide.wow4ever.se/en/instances) · [WoWCensus entry](https://wowcensus.io/forever/npc/tunnelweb-matriarch/)
