# Aeternum 0.6.0 live QA findings

On 28 November 2026, a level-18 Alliance Shaman tested the map on WoW Forever 1.60.1, build 71139. All 302 bosses across 34 dungeons and wings opened from **Boss loot** and displayed their Tank, Healer and DPS guidance. The test character was outdoors with no assigned group role. The session exercised the map UI. Verifying fight timings and behavior in combat requires encounter observations. [Tested release](https://github.com/0xAell/Aeternum/releases/tag/v0.6.0) · [Per-boss test results](0.6.0-encounters.tsv)

## Alliance Shaman trainers have no lessons to preview before a visit

On an Alliance Shaman with no saved visit to these NPCs, open the listed map and click the trainer pin. All four pins opened and showed “Visit this trainer to record their lessons.” A player planning a trip cannot see which spells or ranks are available or how much training costs. These four are among the 39 mapped trainers that still need a recorded catalogue in 0.6.0. [Trainer catalogue coverage](../trainer-data/README.md)

| Trainer | NPC ID | Map | Coordinates (%) |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Teo Hammerstorm | 257446 | Dun Morogh | 28.79, 66.18 |
| Ingrid Dunwald | 258113 | Dun Morogh | 47.58, 51.95 |
| Eldrun Stormbreaker | 258098 | Ironforge | 47.10, 13.79 |
| Farseer Maret Firetend | 270263 | The Hinterlands | 14.40, 42.60 |

To close this gap, capture each NPC’s Forever trainer window and retain the spell IDs, ranks, requirements and any quoted prices with their reputation standing when available. On a character with no saved visit, compare the map menu with that NPC’s recorded offers. Where the price is unknown, the menu should say so. ## The Tactics tooltip can cover the top of its submenu

With the map in its smaller layout, place the pointer near the bottom of the screen, type `/aeternum Sneed`, select **Sneed’s Shredder**, and hover **Tactics**. In the 2940 × 1912 capture from the clean release, the full tactics tooltip covered the upper submenu rows. **Healer TLDR**, **DPS TLDR** and **Recorded Forever casts** remained visible below it. The preview correctly displayed Tremor Totem, Terrify and the blue Magic label. Mouse access to the obscured rows still needs a separate check.

Retest the same menu near the bottom edge and in the middle of the screen. A player should be able to read and reach each class action and role row by mouse while moving from the boss menu into **Tactics**.

## Eight guide-listed items have unverified character requirements

The loot filter omits them from every character’s boss menu. The live test confirmed that the menus followed the shipped filter. ## Regression results

- All 906 individual role tooltips passed. The Shaman saw 356 learned-spell recommendations across 207 bosses. The other 95 showed role guidance. All 481 recorded boss–spell pairs and 357 reference pairs opened native spell tooltips. Automated tests exercised all nine classes.
- All 277 mapped bosses opened from their skulls, including 17 selections from shared skulls. When retesting Sneed’s Shredder, Scarlet Commander Mograine or Warchief Rend Blackhand, select the clickable member row. Its text matches the heading in that menu.
- All 71 floors loaded. Across 540 map views, the game displayed 2,268 entrance placements with zero overlapping entrance hitboxes. Zone level labels resolved for all 44 supported outdoor zones.
- Escape closed the open boss menu and the dungeon map. The client reported zero Aeternum Lua or protected-action errors during the Escape check. All 117 regression tests passed on both Lua 5.1 and LuaJIT.
