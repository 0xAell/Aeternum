Press **M** in **World of Warcraft: Forever** to browse dungeon floors, boss tactics and loot, trainer lessons, and quests on the world map. Aeternum uses Blizzard’s map, menus, and tooltips. Its database is included and it runs on its own.

**Dungeon atlas**

Open **Dungeon atlas** from the world map’s tracking menu. Scroll to zoom toward the pointer, drag across zones, or middle-click to find your character. At close zoom, the atlas blends in WoW’s terrain art for Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms. Click any dungeon entrance for its floor maps or right-click for minimap directions. Returning from a floor restores the same atlas view.

Dungeon floors also zoom smoothly. On supported floors, use **Terrain detail** in the floor menu to switch to in-game minimap artwork while keeping boss pins and your current view in place.

**Dungeons, boss tactics and loot**

Click a dungeon entrance to open its floor map, then hover a boss skull for tactics and loot side by side. Each guide includes tank, healer, and DPS tactics, class abilities your character knows, color-coded effects, and spells. The boss’s creature type, rank, and named adds appear when documented. Recorded Forever casts and reference spells are marked separately.

A shared skull shows every encounter at that spot. Move onto the guide to scroll or hover an item for its stats. Gear and recipes are filtered by your class, proficiencies, and professions. Click a skull for item links and previews. For bosses without a mapped position, open **Boss loot** in the floor menu or search by name.

Aeternum covers 302 encounters across 34 dungeons and wings and 71 floors. The guide marks estimated boss positions. Item tooltips throughout the game show verified boss drops and encounter rolls.

**Dungeons by level**, in the map’s tracking menu and the minimap’s addon compartment, lists every dungeon from the lowest level range to the highest, colored for your character the way the game colors quests.

Right-click an entrance for minimap directions. Ten entrances have mapped approach waypoints. Other doors show a direct bearing. Zoom in to separate nearby entrances.

**Trainers and quests**

Aeternum automatically detects your character’s class, race, and learned professions to tailor boss advice, loot, trainers, and quests.

Your class, faction, professions, and weapon skills determine which trainers appear across 562 locations. Click a trainer to browse lessons, ranks, requirements, and prices. 522 of 561 trainers have offline catalogues before your first visit. Your own visit updates the lessons and prices for that character.

Quest markers show available starts and turn-ins. Following a quest also shows recorded locations for its unfinished objectives. Use the map’s tracking menu to change what appears.

Dungeon and loot research includes work by Exehn, author of DungeonJournal.

**AI disclosure**

AI tools helped brainstorm features and explore design ideas and visual references. They also helped generate and revise Lua code, draft placeholder copy, encounter notes, and documentation, and edit promotional materials. OpenAI ImageGen generated the arch logo used as the project icon. Gameplay screenshots and footage were captured in WoW Forever.

The dungeon atlas connects directly to Aeternum’s encounter guides. Each guide brings role tactics, class and racial counters, and gear or recipes your character can use together on the boss’s floor map. The addon includes its game data for offline use.
